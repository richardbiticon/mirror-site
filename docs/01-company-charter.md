## What Mirror Is

Mirror builds private AI clones of a company's highest-value customer segments, calibrated on their data, deployed in their stack, and owned by them.

Companies use their Mirror to pre-test ads, emails, offers, landing pages, and pricing against the customer who actually pays them. They run focus groups at 3am. They brief their teams with insight no agency can match. They make decisions faster and with less risk.

Mirror is sold as three offers: Mirror/Recon (a 14-day diagnostic), Mirror/Install (a 60-day build with monthly retainer), and Mirror/Operate (fully operated by us, monthly).

## What Mirror Is Not

We are not an AI marketing agency. We don't run paid ads, manage social, or write content for clients (unless they buy Mirror/Operate, in which case Mirror is the asset doing the work, not us).

We are not a research firm. We don't deliver 100-page reports. We deliver a working asset.

We are not a software product. There is no self-serve. There is no free trial. Every Mirror is built and calibrated by humans for a specific company.

We are not a focus group replacement, though we displace some focus group budget. We are a new category that didn't exist before AI made it possible.

We are not a ChatGPT wrapper. The Mirror methodology requires data ingestion, multi-persona architecture, weekly retraining, and human calibration. None of that is a wrapper.

## The Customer

### ICP (in priority order)

**Tier 1: DTC ecommerce brands, $5M to $50M annual revenue, currently spending $30K+ per month on paid acquisition.** These companies feel the cost of being wrong about customers most acutely because every wrong creative or wrong audience costs them in the ad auction. Founder-led or with a marketing director who reports to the founder.

**Tier 2: B2B SaaS, post-Series-A, $5M to $30M ARR, with a defined ICP they're trying to expand within.** They have customer data and want to use it better. They have CMOs or VPs of Marketing who are accountable for pipeline.

**Tier 3: Info-product and creator businesses, $5M+ revenue, with a clear premium offer.** Course creators, agency operators, niche media. They have audience data and conversion problems.

### Outside ICP (do not pursue)

Companies under $5M revenue. Pre-revenue startups. Agencies (they will resell us, which we don't want yet). Enterprises over $200M (different sales motion, not ready). Local services businesses. E-commerce on marketplaces only (Amazon-only, Etsy-only).

### Who at the company we sell to

Founder for tier 1 and tier 3. Head of Marketing or CMO for tier 2. Never a junior marketer. Never an agency on behalf of a brand. The buyer must be someone who can write a $4,500 check without asking permission.

## The Three Offers

### Mirror/Recon

**Price:** $4,500, one-time.
**Duration:** 14 days from kickoff to delivery.
**Deliverable:** A lightweight Mirror built from public and lightly-shared data, plus the Truth Report (20 pages of synthesized customer insight), plus a 60-minute live session where the client interviews their own Mirror.
**Purpose:** Low-friction yes that demonstrates the methodology and converts to Mirror/Install at 30%+ rate.
**Guarantee:** If the client doesn't get at least 3 actionable insights they didn't have before, full refund.

### Mirror/Install

**Price:** $18,000 build fee plus $6,500 per month retainer (12-month minimum).
**Duration:** 60 days to deploy, then ongoing.
**Deliverable:** A fully calibrated Mirror with the client's CRM, sales calls, support tickets, reviews, and 8 first-party customer interviews ingested. Custom-branded interface. Multi-persona architecture. Weekly Mirror Reports. Unlimited team access.
**Purpose:** The bread and butter offer. Most clients live here.
**Guarantee:** If we don't deliver a calibrated Mirror by day 60, the build fee is refunded and the retainer doesn't start.

### Mirror/Operate

**Price:** $15,000 to $25,000 per month, scoped to client.
**Duration:** Quarterly engagements, ongoing.
**Deliverable:** Mirror/Install, plus we operate it. Daily use of Mirror to pre-test campaigns. Briefs to the client's agency or team. A monthly Strategic Foresight document. Effectively a fractional CMO whose superpower is owning the most calibrated voice-of-customer asset in their category.
**Purpose:** Highest-leverage tier for clients doing $20M+ who don't have internal AI capability and want us in the seat.
**Guarantee:** Quarterly KPI agreement. If we miss, next quarter is at 50%.

## Why Mirror Wins

We have answers to the four objections that kill most AI service companies right now.

**"I could just use ChatGPT myself."**
ChatGPT will roleplay a generic customer. Mirror is calibrated on the client's CRM, call recordings, reviews, with multi-persona voting and weekly retraining. The output quality gap is enormous and we demonstrate it in the Diagnostic Call.

**"This is just AI hype, it won't work."**
We show, not tell. Every Diagnostic Call includes a live demo of a Mirror we've built for a recognizable brand. The prospect watches their market get x-rayed before we make a single claim.

**"What if you go out of business?"**
The Mirror lives in their stack. They own it. We deliver IP transfer documents at the end of every Install. They can fire us and keep the asset.

**"This sounds expensive."**
Compared to one bad creative test, one wrong campaign, or one quarter of guessing, Mirror pays for itself in weeks. We have a CFO-friendly ROI calculation in every proposal.

## The Founding Story (90 words)

We built Mirror because we got tired of watching companies make million-dollar decisions based on what they hoped their customer wanted. AI finally made it possible to clone a customer. Not a generic persona. A calibrated, multi-persona, data-grounded clone you can talk to, test against, and learn from. We built it for ourselves first, then realized every company we knew needed one. So we made it the company.

Two founders. Eight AI roles. One thesis: companies that can talk to their customer before they sell to them will win the next decade.
